Before you buy

What the premium is paying for

A coin's price is the metal, plus a making charge (1% to 8% depending on the seller), plus 3% GST on the lot. Refiners such as MMTC-PAMP usually charge the lowest premium; big jewellers charge more and offer exchange against jewellery later. Buyback terms matter as much as the headline price: a seller that buys back at the day's rate with no deduction is worth a slightly higher premium. Check the hallmark (BIS, with HUID) and the purity stamp (999 or 999.9, not 995, unless priced for it) before paying.

Prices are read from each seller's public page at the time shown and are the seller's advertised price including GST; they can change within the day and may differ by delivery city or card offer. The benchmark is the IBJA 999 gold rate (India Bullion and Jewellers Association), published on trading days, per 10 grams before GST; 3% GST is added for the premium comparison. If IBJA is unavailable the page falls back to COMEX gold converted at the interbank USDINR rate plus 6% customs duty, and says so. Marketplaces and app-only sellers are listed with a link only.
